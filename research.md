My research lies in **combinatorial and geometric group theory**, particularly the study of groups acting on rooted trees.

## Branch groups

I study branch groups, self-similar groups, and automata groups, focusing on their structural, geometric, and algorithmic properties. My published work with K. V. Krishna investigates non-contracting branch groups and non-torsion rigid kernels.

- [A branch group in a class of non-contracting weakly regular branch groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.06393)
- [A class of non-contracting branch groups with non-torsion rigid kernels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.04112)

I am also interested in connections between group actions, dynamics, topology, and the asymptotic and algebraic aspects of groups.
