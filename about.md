I am a **National Postdoctoral Fellow (NPDF) at IISER Mohali**, working with **Prof. Mahender Singh** since September 2026.

My research is in combinatorial and geometric group theory, with a particular focus on **branch groups**, self-similar groups, automata groups, and groups acting on rooted trees.

I completed my **PhD in Mathematics at IIT Guwahati in March 2026**, under the supervision of **Prof. K. V. Krishna**. From April to September 2026, I was a Research Associate at **IISER Thiruvananthapuram**, working with **Prof. Viji Z. Thomas**.

## Education

- **MSc in Mathematics and Scientific Computing**, NIT Warangal, 2017–2019.
- **BSc (Honours) in Mathematics**, Dinhata College, University of North Bengal, 2014–2017.
